About & Use

What this web edition is, how it was made, and how it may be used.

The book

Academic Writing and Research — A Practical Guide for International Students in Korean Universities

Written by Yong-Jik Lee, Ph.D., Assistant Professor at Changwon National University. The volume runs to 57 pages and sixteen chapters, moving from entering an academic community, through building each section of a manuscript, to sharing research and sustaining a writing practice.

Fidelity to the source

This edition reproduces the author's PDF as printed. No sentence was added, removed, or rewritten. Three things were done:

  • Paragraphs broken across PDF layout lines were rejoined.
  • Running heads and page numbers were removed.
  • Section headings, learning objectives, key terms, applications, and reflection questions were marked up as structured blocks.

An automated check confirmed 100% word-level preservation across all sixteen chapters (zero words missing, zero broken links).

The Worked revision sections are set as a two-column “Weak / Stronger” table in the print original. The web edition reproduces that text in sequence and notes the difference in each chapter.

How to read

  • Progress bar — the top rule shows how far you have read in the chapter.
  • Dark mode — the ◐ button; it follows your system setting by default.
  • Checklist — marks are stored in your browser only, never uploaded.
  • Keyboard — ← and → move between chapters.
  • Print — printing hides navigation and keeps the text alone.

Use and attribution

Copyright in the text remains with the author. Please cite the source volume when quoting, and contact the author for reuse beyond personal study or classroom use.

The web edition is a static site with no trackers, no analytics, and no external requests. It works offline.
