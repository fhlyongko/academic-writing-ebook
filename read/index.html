About the book

Academic writing is not merely formal English. It is a disciplined practice of making claims, evidence, methods, and limits visible to a scholarly community.

This open web edition supports international students who conduct research and write academic texts at Korean universities. It is designed for undergraduate research papers, capstone projects, graduate theses, and early scholarly publications. Across sixteen chapters, the guide connects transferable principles of research and writing with the institutional expectations that students encounter in departments, courses, laboratories, and supervisory relationships.

Read the chapters in sequence for a complete pathway, or enter at the stage that matches your current project. Each chapter includes learning objectives, key terms, applied guidance, a completion checklist, and reflection questions.
